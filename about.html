Our Story

GEAN comes from Gab, Erhica, Aji, and Norella — the founders committed to helping students reach their goals.

MISSION

To bring everyone’s enthusiasm in seeking information. We aim to let the clientele discover new things and learn deeper to fill a missing gap. Our organization’s goal is to fulfill your goal.

VISION

To create a bridge between users and information by providing appropriate resources for every need. We support each user in achieving their goals through accessible and meaningful services.

Our Team

The platform was designed with simplicity, accessibility, and user experience in mind. We chose this layout and design approach to make navigation more intuitive and efficient for students, allowing them to access resources, information, and features with minimal effort.

Throughout the development process, our team worked closely together to plan how the website would function, ensuring that every section would be easy to understand and navigate. By combining our ideas and experiences, we created a platform that not only serves its purpose but also provides a smooth and user-friendly experience for every student who uses it.
