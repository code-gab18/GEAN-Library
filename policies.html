Library Policies

Everything you need to know about using GEAN Library responsibly and effectively.

Membership & Registration

Anyone in the community can join. Register with valid ID and address. Members receive a library card for borrowing and digital access.

Reserved Items & Holds

Place holds on borrowed items and pick them up within 7 days once notified.

Using the Library

Maintain quiet zones. Study rooms are limited to 2 hours. Computers are first-come, first-served.

Behavior & Respect

Respect others and property. Light snacks allowed in designated areas only.

Children & Families

Children must be supervised. Programs and accessibility support are available.

Digital Services

Access e-books, audiobooks, and research databases anytime.

Lost or Damaged Items

Borrowers are responsible for replacement or repair costs.

Events & Programs

Join workshops and exhibitions. Some events require registration.

Privacy & Security

Your records are confidential and securely stored.

Fees & Payments

Pay fines in-library or online. Assistance available if needed.

Library Hours

Closed on holidays and maintenance days. Updates posted regularly.

Policy Updates

Policies are reviewed and updated periodically.
