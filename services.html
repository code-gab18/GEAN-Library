Library Services

We are committed to providing open-access facilities that bridge the gap between you and the knowledge you seek.

What We Offer

How We Support You

Comprehensive services designed for every stage of your information journey.

🎓

User Orientation

It's important for the clientele to know the rules of the library and how to act as an information literate. Our library provides each user with knowledge about library ethics and guides them in their use of our facilities, ensuring every visit is productive and respectful.

🔄

Circulation Services

We track every item that comes in and out of the library. It is our duty to ensure the collection remains complete and well-maintained. When you borrow an item, our circulation desk handles the process seamlessly from check-out to return.

🔍

Reference Service

If you're in need of assistance finding the right book or resource, our reference service is perfect for you. With a librarian's expert assistance, the clientele will surely know where to look or seek the information they need.

🌐

Online Resource Services

GEAN Library opens all of its online resources for the convenience of users. Making it more accessible and time-efficient. Through our online resources platform, users gain access to a vast array of digital databases, e-books, and academic journals in a single click.

🤝

Community Activities

To help more users and clientele, community activities are regularly held. These events let a wider variety of people learn about the importance of the library. Through community activities, people become more connected to the information the library has to offer.
